My top 3 movies of all time

A Few Good Men (1992)

Director: Rob Reiner
Stars: Tom Cruise, Jack Nicholson, Demi Moore
Genre: Drama, Thriller

Great movie with a great cast. The story is about a military lawyer who defends two U.S. Marines charged with killing a fellow Marine at the Guantanamo Bay Naval Base in Cuba. The case is a court-martial that uncovers a high-level conspiracy. The movie is known for its famous line, "You can't handle the truth!" delivered by Jack Nicholson.

Blood Diamond (2006)

Director: Edward Zwick
Stars: Leonardo DiCaprio, Jennifer Connelly, Djimon Hounsou
Genre: Action, Drama, Thriller

Blood Diamond is a political war thriller film that follows the story of a fisherman, a smuggler, and a syndicate of businessmen who are all searching for a priceless diamond in Sierra Leone during the civil war in 1999. The film explores the issues of conflict diamonds and the impact of the diamond trade on the local population.
